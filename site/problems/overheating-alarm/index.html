Why is my outboard overheating or sounding the alarm?

Overheat alarm / warning · Symptom: Buzzer, overheat light, engine limits RPM.

Quick answer: Most often: blocked water intake, failed impeller, stuck thermostat. Start here: Throttle down, neutral, shut off. Don't "baby it home." If that doesn't fix it, work through the checks below, and see a marine mechanic if you're unsure.

Likely causes (most to least common)

  1. Blocked water intake
  2. Failed impeller
  3. Stuck thermostat
  4. Clogged cooling passages
  5. Exhaust gases entering cooling (head gasket)
  6. Gauge/voltage fault

Safe owner checks & fixes

  1. Throttle down, neutral, shut off. Don't "baby it home."
  2. Clear intakes and telltale (see 06).
  3. Restart only if strong steady stream and alarm clears.
  4. If alarm returns, get a tow.
  5. Steam or spurting from telltale may signal head gasket — mechanic.

Parts / tools

Wire, flush muffs/attachment.

Call a marine mechanic when

Alarm returns after clearing intakes; engine ran hot for more than a few seconds; steam/spurting telltale or white smoke (possible head gasket). Get a tow rather than run it. If you're on the water and unsafe, call TowBoatUS/Sea Tow or the Coast Guard (VHF 16).
