Why has my outboard lost power or won't plane?

Loss of power / won't plane · Symptom: Runs but sluggish, won't reach plane or normal RPM.

Quick answer: Most often: trim too far in/out, too much load or poor weight distribution, damaged or wrong-pitch prop, spun hub, fuel restriction. Start here: Check for warning lights first (overheat/oil/low-pressure modes cut power). If that doesn't fix it, work through the checks below, and see a marine mechanic if you're unsure.

Likely causes (most to least common)

  1. Trim too far in/out, too much load or poor weight distribution
  2. Damaged or wrong-pitch prop, spun hub
  3. Fuel restriction
  4. Fouled plugs
  5. Overheat protection mode
  6. Weed on prop/gearcase, fouled hull bottom

Safe owner checks & fixes

  1. Check for warning lights first (overheat/oil/low-pressure modes cut power).
  2. Inspect prop for damage/debris; check for weeds.
  3. Trim fully down to start, trim up once on plane.
  4. Redistribute weight forward.
  5. Fuel filter/plugs.
  6. Compare WOT RPM to manual's range with a tach; out of range = prop selection or engine issue.

Parts / tools

Prop wrench, spare prop, cotter pin/nut, tach.

Call a marine mechanic when

Can't reach the manual's WOT RPM range with a good prop; engine is in a warning/limp mode you can't clear; prop selection or engine height changes. If you're on the water and unsafe, call TowBoatUS/Sea Tow or the Coast Guard (VHF 16).
