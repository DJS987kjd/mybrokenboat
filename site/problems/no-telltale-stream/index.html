Why is there no tell-tale (pee) stream from my outboard?

No or weak tell-tale (pee) stream · Symptom: Little or no water from the indicator outlet.

Quick answer: Most often: telltale outlet plugged by debris/insects/salt, lower-unit water intake screens blocked (weeds, mud, bag), worn/failed water pump impeller. Start here: Shut engine off promptly if alarm or no stream. If that doesn't fix it, work through the checks below, and see a marine mechanic if you're unsure.

Likely causes (most to least common)

  1. Telltale outlet plugged by debris/insects/salt
  2. Lower-unit water intake screens blocked (weeds, mud, bag)
  3. Worn/failed water pump impeller
  4. Thermostat stuck
  5. Internal passage blockage

Safe owner checks & fixes

  1. Shut engine off promptly if alarm or no stream.
  2. Poke the telltale outlet with a wire/small zip-tie; restart and watch.
  3. Tilt up and clear intake screens.
  4. If stream stays weak/absent: stop running; impeller replacement is moderate DIY with a service manual, otherwise mechanic.

Parts / tools

Stiff wire or zip-tie, flushing attachment, impeller kit (model-specific).

Call a marine mechanic when

Clearing outlet and intakes doesn't restore a strong stream — impeller/pump job if you lack a service manual or tools; steam or spurting from telltale. If you're on the water and unsafe, call TowBoatUS/Sea Tow or the Coast Guard (VHF 16).
