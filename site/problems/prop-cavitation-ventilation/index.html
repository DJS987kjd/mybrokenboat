Why does my prop slip, ventilate, or cavitate?

Prop ventilation / cavitation (RPM spikes, slips) · Symptom: RPM jumps without more speed, especially in turns or rough water.

Quick answer: Most often: trimmed out too far (ventilation), engine mounted too high on transom, damaged prop or gearcase, missing diffuser ring. Start here: Trim down and re-test. If that doesn't fix it, work through the checks below, and see a marine mechanic if you're unsure.

Likely causes (most to least common)

  1. Trimmed out too far (ventilation)
  2. Engine mounted too high on transom
  3. Damaged prop or gearcase, missing diffuser ring
  4. Weeds/debris on prop (cavitation)
  5. Bent blade or burrs
  6. Spun prop hub

Safe owner checks & fixes

  1. Trim down and re-test.
  2. Remove debris; inspect blades for bends/nicks.
  3. Have damaged prop repaired by a prop shop.
  4. Mercury PVS props: plug sizes tune venting.
  5. Engine height changes: mechanic/dealer.

Parts / tools

Prop wrench, spare prop.

Call a marine mechanic when

Damaged gearcase or skeg; bent prop (prop shop); engine height on transom needs changing; spun hub. If you're on the water and unsafe, call TowBoatUS/Sea Tow or the Coast Guard (VHF 16).
