Why do my outboard spark plugs keep fouling?

Spark plug fouling · Symptom: Misfire, hard start; plugs wet, black or oily.

Quick answer: Most often: long idling/trolling, wrong plug heat range, too rich (choke, carb). Start here: Use plug type in manual (Honda notes wrong heat range can trigger overheat warning). If that doesn't fix it, work through the checks below, and see a marine mechanic if you're unsure.

Likely causes (most to least common)

  1. Long idling/trolling
  2. Wrong plug heat range
  3. Too rich (choke, carb)
  4. Wrong oil mix (2-stroke)

Safe owner checks & fixes

  1. Use plug type in manual (Honda notes wrong heat range can trigger overheat warning).
  2. Gap per manual.
  3. Avoid prolonged idle; clear out with periodic runs at speed if manual allows.
  4. Persistent oily fouling: mechanic.

Parts / tools

Plug socket, gap tool, plugs per manual.

Call a marine mechanic when

New correct plugs foul again quickly; white/blistered plugs (overheating — check cooling); oily plugs on a 4-stroke. If you're on the water and unsafe, call TowBoatUS/Sea Tow or the Coast Guard (VHF 16).
