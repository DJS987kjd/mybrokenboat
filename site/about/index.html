What is My Broken Boat?

My Broken Boat (Outboard Help) collects common outboard motor fixes from owner's manuals and manufacturer resources and explains them in plain English, so you can try the safe, simple checks first and know when it's time for a pro.

Every page lists its sources. Specs vary by make, model, and year, so your owner's manual always wins. We don't sell parts, and the site has no ads, affiliate links, or tracking.
