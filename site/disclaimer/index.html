Disclaimer

My Broken Boat provides general, informational troubleshooting tips for outboard motors. It is not a substitute for your engine's owner's manual, a factory service manual, or a qualified marine technician.

  • Specifications, intervals, and procedures vary by make, model, and year. Always follow your owner's manual.
  • Working on engines, fuel systems, and electrical systems carries risk of fire, explosion, injury, and property damage. Gasoline vapor is explosive; never work on a fuel system near sparks or flame.
  • If you smell fuel, see a leak, hear internal engine noise, or have any doubt, stop and contact a qualified marine mechanic.
  • On the water, safety comes first: if you're in danger, call the Coast Guard (VHF channel 16) or a towing service.
  • Doing your own repairs may affect your warranty.
