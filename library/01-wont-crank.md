# Electric start: won't crank (no click / click only)

**Symptom:** Turn key; nothing happens or only a click.

## Likely causes (most to least common)
1. Kill-switch lanyard not attached
2. Not in neutral (neutral safety switch)
3. Weak/dead battery
4. Loose or corroded battery cables / ground
5. Blown fuse
6. Faulty starter relay/solenoid or starter motor

## Safe owner checks & fixes
1. Confirm lanyard clip is seated on the stop switch.
2. Shift firmly into neutral and retry.
3. Check battery charge with a voltmeter; charge or swap if low (correct voltage: check your owner's manual / battery label).
4. Clean and tighten both battery terminals and the engine ground.
5. Check fuses per owner's manual "Replacing fuse" section.
6. Manual-start models or emergency: owner's manuals describe an emergency rope start procedure — follow yours exactly.

## Parts / tools
Voltmeter/multimeter, wrench set, wire brush, spare fuses (rating per manual), battery charger.

## Call a marine mechanic when
Battery is good and connections clean but still only clicks (starter/solenoid); any burning smell, hot or melted wiring; repeated fuse blowing. If you're on the water and unsafe, call TowBoatUS/Sea Tow or the Coast Guard (VHF 16).
