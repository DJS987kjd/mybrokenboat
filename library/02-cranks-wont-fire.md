# Cranks but won't start

**Symptom:** Engine turns over normally but never catches.

## Likely causes (most to least common)
1. Fuel not reaching engine: tank vent closed, empty tank, line not connected, primer bulb not pumped
2. Clogged fuel filter / tank filter
3. Stale or contaminated fuel
4. Wrong choke/enrichment use (flooded or not enriched)
5. Fouled/wet spark plugs or bad gap
6. Ignition fault (coil, sensor, ECU) or kill-switch wiring

## Safe owner checks & fixes
1. Open tank vent; confirm fuel level; reconnect hose fittings at both ends.
2. Squeeze primer bulb until firm (see 15-primer-bulb).
3. Check/replace fuel filter per manual.
4. Check fuel in a clear jar for water/separation (see 10).
5. Pull plugs: wet = flooded (crank with throttle per manual's flooded procedure); dry = fuel delivery problem.
6. Inspect/replace plugs with manual-specified type and gap.

## Parts / tools
Plug socket, spare plugs (type per manual), spare fuel filter, clear jar, fresh fuel.

## Call a marine mechanic when
Fuel and plugs are confirmed good but no spark; you smell raw fuel in the cowl; EFI warning codes; low compression suspected after overheating. If you're on the water and unsafe, call TowBoatUS/Sea Tow or the Coast Guard (VHF 16).
