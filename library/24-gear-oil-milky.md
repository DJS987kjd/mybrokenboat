# Milky gear oil / water in lower unit

**Symptom:** Creamy/white gear lube when drained.

## Likely causes (most to least common)
1. Prop shaft seal damaged by fishing line
2. Worn seals
3. Drain/vent screw gasket bad

## Safe owner checks & fixes
1. Check prop shaft for fishing line.
2. Have dealer pressure-test gearcase.
3. Replace gear lube.

## Parts / tools
Gear lube pump, gaskets, prop wrench.

## Call a marine mechanic when
Always: gearcase pressure/vacuum test and seal replacement; metal flakes in lube. If you're on the water and unsafe, call TowBoatUS/Sea Tow or the Coast Guard (VHF 16).
