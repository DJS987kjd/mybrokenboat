# Primer bulb soft / won't firm up

**Symptom:** Bulb stays squishy or collapses.

## Likely causes (most to least common)
1. Air leak: loose connector or bad O-ring at quick-connect
2. Failed check valves in bulb (installed backwards or worn)
3. Cracked hose or bulb
4. Empty tank / pickup out of fuel
5. Collapsed bulb = clogged vent or pickup

## Safe owner checks & fixes
1. Bulb arrow points toward engine.
2. Reseat fittings; inspect O-rings.
3. Collapsing bulb: open tank vent.
4. Replace bulb/line if cracked or won't hold.
5. Fuel dripping at fittings: fix before running.

## Parts / tools
Primer bulb kit (correct hose size), stainless clamps, O-rings.

## Call a marine mechanic when
Leak at the engine-side fitting or fuel pump; bulb new and correctly fitted but still won't firm (suspect pickup or anti-siphon valve in tank). If you're on the water and unsafe, call TowBoatUS/Sea Tow or the Coast Guard (VHF 16).
