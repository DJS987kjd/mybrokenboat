# Stalls or bogs when throttling up

**Symptom:** Idles OK but stumbles/dies on acceleration.

## Likely causes (most to least common)
1. Fuel starvation: clogged filter, vent closed, kinked line, weak bulb
2. Gummed carb main/accelerator circuit
3. Water in fuel
4. Fouled plugs
5. Fuel pump fault (dealer)

## Safe owner checks & fixes
1. Open vent, straighten hose, pump bulb while accelerating — improvement = supply issue.
2. Replace fuel filter.
3. Check fuel for water.
4. Replace plugs.
5. Still bogging: carb or fuel pump service by mechanic.

## Parts / tools
Filter, fuel line, plugs.

## Call a marine mechanic when
Persists after filter, fuel and plugs; any fuel pump replacement on EFI (high-pressure fuel); suspected carb accelerator circuit. If you're on the water and unsafe, call TowBoatUS/Sea Tow or the Coast Guard (VHF 16).
