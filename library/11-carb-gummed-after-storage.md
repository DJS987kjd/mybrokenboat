# Carburetor gummed after storage

**Symptom:** Hard start, runs only on choke, surges after sitting months.

## Likely causes (most to least common)
1. Old fuel evaporated, leaving varnish in jets
2. Fuel left in carb bowl without stabilizer
3. Ethanol attacking fuel parts

## Safe owner checks & fixes
1. Drain carb bowl (manual procedure) and refill with fresh fuel.
2. Try a fuel-system cleaner per label.
3. If no improvement, carb removal/ultrasonic cleaning or rebuild — skilled DIY or mechanic.
4. Prevention: stabilizer + run 10 min, then run carb dry/drain (Honda).

## Parts / tools
Screwdrivers, carb cleaner, rebuild kit (model-specific), fresh fuel.

## Call a marine mechanic when
Cleaner and fresh fuel don't help and you aren't comfortable removing/rebuilding the carb; multi-carb engines needing synchronisation. If you're on the water and unsafe, call TowBoatUS/Sea Tow or the Coast Guard (VHF 16).
