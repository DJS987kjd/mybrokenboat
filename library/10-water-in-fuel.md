# Fuel smell / water in fuel

**Symptom:** Sputtering, stalls, fuel odor, water seen in filter/separator.

## Likely causes (most to least common)
1. Condensation in vented tank
2. Ethanol phase separation
3. Leaking fuel fitting/line (odor)
4. Rainwater via cap or vent

## Safe owner checks & fixes
1. FUEL SMELL: stop, no sparks/smoking, ventilate, find leak before running.
2. Drain a few ounces into a clear glass jar, let sit ~30 min; layer at bottom = water/phase separation (Mercury).
3. Drain water separator/filter per manual.
4. Phase-separated fuel cannot be fixed with additives — remove fuel (professional removal recommended for built-in tanks).
5. Install/replace a fuel-water separator.

## Parts / tools
Clear jar, water-finding paste, separator filter, absorbent pads.

## Call a marine mechanic when
Phase separation in a built-in tank (professional removal); any fuel leak you can't fix by replacing an external hose/fitting; fuel in the bilge. If you're on the water and unsafe, call TowBoatUS/Sea Tow or the Coast Guard (VHF 16).
