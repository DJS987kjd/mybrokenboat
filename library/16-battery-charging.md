# Battery and charging problems

**Symptom:** Battery goes dead, slow cranking, gauges flicker.

## Likely causes (most to least common)
1. Loose battery cables (vibration loosens nuts) — techs say most engine problems are fuel or battery
2. Corroded terminals
3. Battery left connected/discharging between trips
4. Old battery (Discover Boating: expect ~4–5 seasons from a well-kept marine starting battery)
5. Automotive battery used instead of marine
6. Charging system (stator/rectifier/regulator) fault

## Safe owner checks & fixes
1. Wiggle each cable at the terminal — it should not budge; tighten if it does.
2. Clean corrosion (Discover Boating suggests baking soda and water); fit a boot on the positive terminal.
3. Make sure the battery is secured in a box or tray.
4. Use a marine maintenance charger between outings; turn battery switch off for storage.
5. Have the battery load-tested at a parts store.
6. If a good battery keeps going flat while running, suspect the charging system (expected charging output is model-specific — check your owner's manual).

## Parts / tools
Multimeter, terminal brush, baking soda, terminal boot, marine battery maintainer/charger, replacement marine battery (spec per manual).

## Call a marine mechanic when
Battery tests good but keeps going flat when running (stator/rectifier/regulator); melted or heat-damaged wiring; any battery that is swollen, cracked or leaking. If you're on the water and unsafe, call TowBoatUS/Sea Tow or the Coast Guard (VHF 16).
