# Rough idle / surging at idle

**Symptom:** Idles unevenly, hunts, or shakes.

## Likely causes (most to least common)
1. Gummed carb idle circuit (after storage)
2. Fouled plug / misfiring cylinder
3. Air leak in fuel supply
4. Water/stale fuel
5. Idle speed out of spec
6. Blocked idle exhaust relief (some models)

## Safe owner checks & fixes
1. Try fresh fuel with stabilizer/cleaner per label.
2. Inspect and replace plugs.
3. Check fuel connections and bulb.
4. Check idle speed per manual.
5. If persists after fuel/plugs, carb cleaning or EFI diagnosis by a mechanic.

## Parts / tools
Plugs, fuel filter, fresh fuel, fuel system cleaner.

## Call a marine mechanic when
Rough after fresh fuel and plugs (carb or EFI injector service); one cylinder consistently dead; knocking noise. If you're on the water and unsafe, call TowBoatUS/Sea Tow or the Coast Guard (VHF 16).
