# Ethanol (E10) fuel problems

**Symptom:** Gummy deposits, clogged filters soon after switching, hard starts.

## Likely causes (most to least common)
1. E10 absorbs water → phase separation
2. E10 solvent action loosens tank debris into filters
3. Incompatible old fiberglass tanks/hoses

## Safe owner checks & fixes
1. Never use above E10 in outboards (Mercury, Honda).
2. Carry spare filters; replace as needed.
3. Add stabilizer; store tank nearly full if vented (or per tank type — Mercury notes low-emission tanks differ).
4. Use ethanol-free fuel where available.
5. Suspected tank incompatibility: contact tank manufacturer.

## Parts / tools
Filters, stabilizer, separator.

## Call a marine mechanic when
Old fiberglass tank may be E10-incompatible; repeated filter clogging; fuel hose softening or leaking. If you're on the water and unsafe, call TowBoatUS/Sea Tow or the Coast Guard (VHF 16).
