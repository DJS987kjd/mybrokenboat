# Kill switch / lanyard issues

**Symptom:** Won't start or cuts out suddenly.

## Likely causes (most to least common)
1. Lanyard clip missing/loose
2. Wrong clip type
3. Corroded/damaged stop switch or wiring

## Safe owner checks & fixes
1. Attach lanyard and clip fully.
2. Carry a spare lanyard.
3. Honda lists stop switch wiring leak / switch not returning as dealer items.
4. Always wear the lanyard — required for safety on many boats.

## Parts / tools
Spare lanyard.

## Call a marine mechanic when
Engine cuts out with lanyard attached (stop switch or wiring short — Honda lists as dealer item); switch doesn't return to run position. If you're on the water and unsafe, call TowBoatUS/Sea Tow or the Coast Guard (VHF 16).
