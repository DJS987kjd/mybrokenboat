# Starts then dies

**Symptom:** Starts, runs seconds to minutes, then stalls.

## Likely causes (most to least common)
1. Tank vent closed (vacuum builds)
2. Primer bulb/check valve failing or air leak in fuel line
3. Partially clogged filter or carb idle jet
4. Choke/enrichment removed too early on cold engine
5. Water in fuel
6. Idle speed set too low

## Safe owner checks & fixes
1. Open vent; loosen cap — if it runs on, vent was the cause.
2. Squeeze bulb while running; if engine recovers, suspect fuel supply.
3. Inspect fuel line, clamps and connectors for cracks or air leaks.
4. Replace fuel filter; check fuel for water.
5. Warm up per manual before removing choke.
6. Idle speed check is in the manual's maintenance section; adjustment may be dealer-only on EFI.

## Parts / tools
Fuel line/bulb kit, filter, screwdrivers, clear jar.

## Call a marine mechanic when
Dies even with vent open, fresh fuel and new filter (fuel pump or carb); fuel leaking from bulb or fittings — stop and fix before running. If you're on the water and unsafe, call TowBoatUS/Sea Tow or the Coast Guard (VHF 16).
